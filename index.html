CURRENT PROGRESS

What is live today.

These notes describe the website as it works now. Later security tools stay listed as commitments until they exist.

Live

Search homepage

GitHub Pages serves this site over HTTPS. Choosing Search opens Google’s results. CryptoGuard does not crawl the web or keep its own index.

Live

Security controls on the page

The page sets a restrictive Content Security Policy and a no-referrer policy. The local preview also blocks framing and turns off camera, microphone, and location access.

Live

Automated checks

A scheduled workflow requests the public homepage and stylesheet over HTTPS and looks for the expected security controls. Dependabot proposes GitHub Actions updates each week.

Published

Mission and values

The mission, eight core values, and the CryptoGuard commitment are on this page. Each commitment names a practice we intend to build. None of them is an audit report.

Not built

Guardian tools

Threat detection, malware scanning, download quarantine, website permission controls, and cryptocurrency wallet protection are not part of this website.

Live

Searches stay off this site

This page does not save what you type. Google receives the words only after you press Search, and then under Google’s own practices.

OUR MISSION

People, security, integrity, and trust before profits.

To empower individuals and communities through advanced cybersecurity, responsible innovation, reliable information, cryptocurrency safety tools, accessible education, and ethical technology.

OUR CORE VALUES

Principles, put into practice.

These are commitments for how we aim to build and operate CryptoGuard. They describe our direction, not completed audits or services.

01

Integrity & Ethics

Act honestly, fairly, and responsibly, guided by truth and the protection of users.

Commitment: publish an ethics policy and independent review standards.

02

Innovation & Continuous Improvement

Learn continuously and improve security, performance, accessibility, and experience.

Commitment: conduct regular security testing and product improvements.

03

Collaboration & Inclusion

Welcome diverse perspectives and make space for everyone to contribute and be heard.

Commitment: build accessible features and invite community feedback.

04

Sustainability & Social Responsibility

Support responsible technology, digital literacy, and the well-being of future generations.

Commitment: use efficient infrastructure and report environmental impact.

05

Accountability

Address mistakes and concerns openly, take responsibility, and keep improving.

Commitment: establish incident disclosure and user complaint procedures.

06

Honor

Uphold commitments with professionalism, courage, fairness, and care.

Commitment: make clear promises and lead responsibly.

07

Respect

Respect human dignity, privacy, individual rights, and cultural differences.

Commitment: protect privacy rights and moderate the community fairly.

08

Trust

Earn confidence through transparent operations and responsible security practices.

Commitment: share security reports and pursue independent audits.

THE CRYPTOGUARD COMMITMENT

Technology should protect, empower, and unite humanity.

We aim to build a safer, more reliable, transparent, and accessible digital world through ethical innovation and responsible leadership. We will place human dignity, privacy, safety, and the public good at the center of our decisions; stay accountable for our actions; and be clear about our limitations.

CryptoGuard — Protecting Digital Freedom. Empowering Humanity. Building Trust.
